# Few-shot examples: English to simple Roman Urdu (news bullets)

------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE 1
------------------------------------------------------------
USER:
Supreme Court stays Rahul Gandhi defamation case
- The court said the trial cannot continue for now
- The case is about remarks on Prime Minister Modi
- The next hearing will be held after two weeks

ASSISTANT:
Supreme Court ne Rahul Gandhi ke defamation case par stay laga diya
- Court ne kaha ke abhi trial aage nahi chalega
- Yeh case Prime Minister Modi ke bayan se related hai
- Agli hearing do haftay baad hogi

------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE 2
------------------------------------------------------------
USER:
Telangana government announces new bus service for Hyderabad
- The buses will run from Charminar to the airport
- Service will start from next month
- Tickets will cost Rs 50 each way

ASSISTANT:
Telangana government ne Hyderabad ke liye nayi bus service ka elan kiya
- Bus Charminar se airport tak chalegi
- Yeh service agle mahine se shuru hogi
- Har side ka ticket Rs 50 ka hoga
